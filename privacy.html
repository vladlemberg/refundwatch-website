Privacy Policy

Draft — not yet effective. This page must be reviewed and completed before public launch. Contact details, retention periods, and actual data handling must be verified.

RefundWatch helps you track refunds you manually register. With your permission, it uses Plaid to connect to a bank account you select and look for incoming refund transactions.

Information we process

Depending on the features you use, this may include refund details you enter (such as merchant, amount, expected date and notes), selected account identifiers, and transaction information needed to identify incoming refunds.

How we use information

We use this information to display your tracked refunds and identify potential matching credits on the account you choose. RefundWatch is read-only and cannot initiate payments, transfers or withdrawals.

Bank connections

Bank connections are provided through Plaid. Plaid's own privacy practices also apply to information processed through its services. You can disconnect a linked account from within RefundWatch.

Security, retention and deletion

We use security measures appropriate to the service. Specific storage, retention, deletion and third-party processing details will be documented here before launch.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, see our Support page. A verified support email will be published before launch.
