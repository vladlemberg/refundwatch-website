Support

Need help with RefundWatch or a connected bank account?

Connecting your bank

RefundWatch uses Plaid to connect to supported financial institutions. If a bank is unavailable, try again later or contact support when the support channel is published.

Disconnecting an account

Use the account management controls in RefundWatch to disconnect a linked bank account.

Refund status

RefundWatch tracks refunds you add. If a refund is missing or delayed, confirm its status with the merchant or your bank.

Contact us

A verified support email address will be added here before launch. Do not submit sensitive financial information through public GitHub issues.
