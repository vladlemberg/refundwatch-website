RefundWatch Support

For account, bank-connection, refund-tracking, privacy or technical questions, email refundwatch.support@gmail.com.

Keep sensitive information private. Do not email passwords, verification codes, Plaid/Firebase access tokens, full account or card numbers, or unredacted financial documents. Do not post personal financial information in public GitHub issues.

Account access

RefundWatch uses Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. Use the same provider account you originally used. For a provider-password or provider-account recovery problem, use Apple’s or Google’s recovery process. RefundWatch support does not need your provider password or verification codes.

Signing out leaves your local refund records on that device. Demo mode uses local illustrative data; it is not a real connected bank account.

Bank connections

Supported bank connections are provided through Plaid. If connection or saving fails, follow the app’s retry or recovery instructions. Availability depends on your financial institution and Plaid; RefundWatch cannot guarantee that a specific institution is supported.

To disconnect, open Banks & cards and use Disconnect. Disconnecting one account revokes its shared bank connection and also disconnects other accounts linked through that same connection. It does not close the actual bank accounts or delete your local refund history. If the operation fails or its outcome is uncertain, follow recovery instructions or contact support.

You can also review connections using Plaid Portal, subject to Plaid’s own options and policies. Never send bank login credentials to support.

Refund tracking

Add the refunds you want to track, choose the appropriate connected account and use Check refunds to request a check for posted credits. RefundWatch does not continuously monitor accounts in the background or initiate payments.

Review possible matches carefully, especially when the bank credit differs from the amount you registered. A manual Received confirmation records your choice; it does not prove the full amount was returned. For a refund that is missing, delayed or disputed, contact the merchant or bank and verify your bank statement.

Privacy questions

Read the Privacy Policy draft for the reviewed data flows and current limitations. Email refundwatch.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. A request may require identity verification; do not send identity documents or sensitive financial information before a safe method has been agreed.

Receipt recognition happens on your device. Settings → Save receipt photos controls new saved images and asks whether to keep existing copies when you turn the setting off.

Account deletion

In the app, open Settings → Delete Account and follow the reauthentication and confirmation steps. Deletion can require retries; use the displayed recovery instructions if it is interrupted or fails.

Signing out or deleting the app does not delete the server account or disconnect a bank. Local files on other devices, backups and provider-retained information may remain; see the deletion explanation. If you cannot sign in or complete deletion, email support for assistance without including passwords or tokens. No particular response time is promised.

Technical problems

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, RefundWatch app version, an approximate time, the steps you took and the error text. If you share a screenshot, first redact names, balances, transaction details, account digits and receipt contents. Do not send bank statements or receipt images unless support has agreed on a safe, necessary way to handle them.
