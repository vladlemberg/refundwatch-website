4. Plaid connections and read-only access

Optional bank connections use Plaid and supported institutions. Their availability and applicable terms are separate from RefundWatch’s. Plaid or your bank handles the bank-authentication flow.

RefundWatch’s banking features are read-only: they do not initiate payments, transfer money, withdraw funds, hold your funds or issue merchant refunds. Banks and merchants process the underlying transactions.

You can request disconnection in Banks & cards. When disconnection completes, it revokes the selected Plaid connection, including every account linked through that connection. RefundWatch does not revoke other Plaid Items merely because they are at the same bank. Disconnection does not close the underlying bank accounts or erase your local refund history.

Separate Plaid sign-ins do not guarantee independent or uninterrupted connectivity. Some institutions can invalidate an earlier connection when another is established. Plaid or your institution may require renewed authorization.

5. Refund tracking and confirmation limitations

RefundWatch tracks refunds you add manually and checks for eligible posted credits when you request a check. It does not continuously monitor your bank in the background. Bank data may be delayed, unavailable, incomplete or outside the supported account, currency or date range.

Disconnected accounts are no longer included in bank-based refund checks. Reconnection may be needed to check an account again; it does not guarantee that existing refund records will be associated with the new connection. Check each refund’s destination account before relying on further checks.

Automatic matching depends on the supported account, merchant, amount, date and uniqueness rules. Ambiguous or amount-mismatched credits require review. If you manually mark a refund Received, the app records your decision; it does not establish that the merchant returned the full registered amount. Partial-refund accounting is not supported by the current app.

No refund guarantee: RefundWatch does not guarantee that a merchant will approve, issue or complete a refund, or that the app will detect every credit. Check important information against your bank records and contact the merchant or bank about a missing or disputed refund. The app is not a substitute for those records or for financial, legal or tax advice.
