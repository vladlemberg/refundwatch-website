About RefundWatch

RefundWatch is a tool for tracking refunds you register manually. It may use transaction information from a bank account you choose to help identify incoming refund credits.

Not a payment service

RefundWatch provides read-only account monitoring. It cannot issue refunds, initiate payments, transfer money, or withdraw funds. Merchants and financial institutions remain responsible for processing refunds.

Matching and availability

Refund matching is informational and may be incomplete or delayed. You should verify important transaction information with your bank or merchant. Access to connected institutions may vary.

Your responsibilities

Use the service only for accounts you are authorized to connect, and provide accurate refund information.
